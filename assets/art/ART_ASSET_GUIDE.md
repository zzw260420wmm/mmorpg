Generated by `scripts/tools/generate_pixel_art_assets.ps1`.

## Atlases

- `res://assets/tiles/urban_life_tileset.png`: reusable 16x16 ground/wall tiles for street, apartment, office, metro, delivery station, and media company.
- `res://assets/sprites/urban_props_atlas.png`: 32x32 props including scooters, rider bags, vending machine, AC unit, laundry rack, signs, ring light, camera, boxes, desk, bed, fridge, and rent notice.
- `res://assets/sprites/characters_atlas.png`: 64x64 hand-drawn cartoon character sprite bases for player, landlord, shopkeeper, drifter girl, delivery rider, office worker, streamer, and metro commuter. Rows are down idle/walk, up idle/walk, left idle/walk, and right idle/walk.
- `res://assets/sprites/cartoon_buildings_atlas.png`: 192x128 non-pixel hand-drawn building sprites for Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Chongqing, Wuhan, and Nanjing.
- `res://assets/ui/hud_icons.png`: compact 16x16 HUD icons for money, energy, stress, rent, weather, work, delivery, and stream.

## Art Direction

The batch follows the project-local art director skill: grounded modern Shanghai, warm gray, muted blue, faded green, tungsten yellow, rainy asphalt, aged concrete, sparse neon, top-down readability, and lived-in economic pressure.

Characters intentionally use smooth 2D hand-drawn cartoon line art rather than pixel art. They should sit over the tile-based world like small illustrated life-sim figures, with clean silhouettes and restrained cel shading.

Outdoor buildings should also avoid hard pixel-block rendering. Keep their footprint snapped to the city grid for navigation, but draw them with soft cartoon shadows, rounded corners, clean line-art outlines, cel-shaded body colors, and simple hand-drawn windows, signs, doors, awnings, and scooters.

Multi-city buildings should share the same hand-drawn rendering language but keep city-specific architectural identity:

- Shanghai: lane houses, humid convenience stores, glass offices, media-company pink light.
- Beijing: hutong gray roofs and red courtyard doors.
- Guangzhou: qilou arcade columns and warm shopfronts.
- Shenzhen: cooler glass tech-park blocks.
- Chengdu: warm food shops and relaxed street-life color.
- Hangzhou: white walls, dark roofs, softer waterside palette.
- Chongqing: slope-side apartments and stepped road pressure.
- Wuhan: riverside markets with humid blue-green notes.
- Nanjing: older blocks shaded by plane trees.

## Reuse Strategy

Prefer reusing the same atlas regions across scenes:

- Rental room: aged floor, warm wall, bed, fridge, rent notice, laundry rack.
- Street: old concrete, wet asphalt, alley patch, scooters, vending machine, utility pole, AC unit.
- Restaurant and delivery loop: restaurant wall, food sign, rider bag, scooter.
- Office: office tile, office desk, muted blue windows.
- Media company: media floor/wall, ring light, camera, product boxes, streamer sprite.
- HUD: use icons only if/when the HUD switches from text-only to icon-assisted status.
